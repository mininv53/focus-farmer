# Privacy

Focus Realm collects **nothing** about you in the MVP. Your spirits, sessions,
badges, and settings are stored entirely in your browser's `localStorage`.
They never leave your device.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no email, no name, no age verification beyond a self-attestation.
- No advertising, no behavioural tracking, no cross-site cookies.
- No paid loot boxes, no gambling-style randomness behind a paywall.
- No FOMO timers or shame mechanics.

## Analytics

The MVP uses **no analytics**. If we later add analytics it will be a
privacy-first, anonymous tool such as [Plausible](https://plausible.io). We
will publish the change in this file and a release note before turning it on.

## Your data, your control

The Settings page exposes:

- **Export JSON** — downloads a `focus-realm-YYYY-MM-DD.json` with everything.
- **Reset realm** — wipes all spirits, sessions, badges, and resources from
  this browser. There is no undo.
